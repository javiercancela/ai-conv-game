# The Last Crossing

A player acts and speaks in a stormbound harbor office to obtain Maren's rescue key.
The Narrator describes the encounter's observable outcomes.

## Language

**Action**:
A physical attempt by the player, such as reading the ledger or reaching for the key.
Declaring an action does not guarantee that it succeeds.

**Speech**:
Words the player explicitly communicates to a character by addressing them, describing
speaking to them, or entering a spoken quotation. Private thoughts and unaddressed
requests or promises are not speech.

**Narrator**:
The voice that describes physical outcomes, visible reactions, and information the
player can perceive. The Narrator does not reveal characters' private thoughts.

**Character reaction**:
A character's observable response to speech or an action. A reaction can be silent
or include spoken dialogue.

**Turn**:
One evaluated player line, containing an action, speech, both, or an unclear intention,
that advances the encounter's clock.
