How the game works

A traveler is stranded beyond the harbor wall. You have twelve turns to act and speak to Maren, the harbor-master, and persuade him to lend you the brass key to the rescue skiff locker.

The setting, characters, objects, and rescue objective are fixed. Your actions and spoken words determine how the encounter develops.

The route to the key

Four moves to a win

The key request is its own step. Build the case first, then ask clearly while Maren is still willing.

01 / DISCOVER

Read the tide ledger

Learn that the east steps are sheltered. The Narrator describes what you read; asking Maren about it does not count.

I read the ledger.
02 / COMMIT

Agree to all three safety steps

Tell Maren you'll take a rope, use the east steps, and return the key. This records plan agreed.

Maren, I'll take a rope, use the east steps, and bring the key back.
03 / REASSURE

Earn Maren's trust

Get trust to at least 55%, keep suspicion below 65%, and avoid escalating tension.

Maren, I want to bring them home safely.
04 / REQUEST

Ask for the key

Once ready, explicitly ask Maren for the brass key. Reaching for it without permission is blocked.

Maren, please lend me the key.

⌛ What uses a turn?

Each evaluated line, including unclear or off-world remarks. You get 12 turns; an early key request can be refused.

Possible endings

Where the conversation leads

The game rules choose the ending. Narration and character dialogue reflect it; generated words cannot award the key.

✓

Key received

Meet every handover condition and ask before the window closes. The rescue can begin.

!

Maren ends the talk

Suspicion reaches 90%, or composure falls to 10% or less. The key stays with him.

⌛

Tide closes the crossing

Reach turn 12 without winning or losing. There is no time left for the rescue.
